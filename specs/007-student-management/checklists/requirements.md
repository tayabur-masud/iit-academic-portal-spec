## Notes

- Supporting paper documents and scans remain outside this feature; only typed form data is recorded.
- A Student profile has one Student-role account and can have separate program admissions. The initial admission creates a pending account; subsequent admissions reuse it without another setup email. Applicant email and mobile are required digital fields. Graduation eligibility is calculated separately for each admission and final-result publication is not gated.
- Profile-level academic-history entries preserve the applicable fields from the first-page form variants and are shared across a person's program admissions; paper certificates, photos, the declaration/signature, identification-card panel, and money-receipt panel remain physical and are not captured, uploaded, or generated by this feature.
- Academic-history Category uses the six fixed values. Listed school/college, university, institution, board, and group values use Configuration references with saved name snapshots; unlisted values use typed text without a reference. Other applicable prior-education fields remain optional text snapshots.
- Mailing and permanent addresses each retain a free-text details value and exactly one Upazila or City Corporation selection under the selected District; District and Division are resolved from the existing geographic hierarchy rather than entered independently.
- Graduation eligibility combines the selected admission's Degree credit and minimum-CGPA requirements, current cumulative CGPA for that admission from published course results, assigned Hall, and registration number. Credits and CGPA do not transfer across admissions. Missing source data prevents eligibility. Student Management consumes CGPA calculated from published results using configured Grade numeric values; it does not calculate or persist CGPA.
