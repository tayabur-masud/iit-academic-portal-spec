## Notes

- Supporting paper documents and scans remain outside this feature; only typed form data is recorded.
- Completing admission creates one linked Student-role account in pending setup; the existing email credential-setup flow handles first sign-in. Applicant email and mobile are required digital fields. Graduation eligibility is calculated automatically from required credits, assigned hall, and registration number; final-result publication is not gated.
- Academic-history entries preserve the applicable fields from the first-page form variants; paper certificates, photos, the declaration/signature, identification-card panel, and money-receipt panel remain physical and are not captured, uploaded, or generated by this feature.
- Graduation eligibility automatically combines the selected degree's required credits, passed course-result credits, assigned hall, and registration number; missing source data prevents eligibility. Student Management does not own degree settings or course/result records.
